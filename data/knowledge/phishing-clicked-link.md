# Clicked a phishing link or entered your password

Speed matters more than anything else. Report it straight away; you will not be blamed for reporting. The Security Team checks the sign-in logs and blocks the attacker's sessions.

## Symptoms
- You typed your company password into a page that came from an email link.
- You opened an attachment that asked you to "enable content" or "enable editing".
- You approved an Authenticator prompt you did not start.

## Resolution
1. Change your password now at https://myaccount.acme-demo.in from a different device if possible.
2. Choose "Sign out everywhere" on the same page so any stolen session is ended.
3. Report the email with Report > Report phishing in Outlook, then do not delete it; the Security Team may need it.
4. If you opened an attachment, disconnect the laptop from Wi-Fi and the network cable, leave it switched on and do not use it further.
5. Raise the ticket from your phone or a colleague's device and mention the time you clicked.

## Escalation criteria
This is always escalated to the Security Team as a priority incident. Also tell your manager when:
- The account belongs to Finance, HR or an administrator.
- You approved an unexpected Authenticator prompt (the attacker may already be signed in).
