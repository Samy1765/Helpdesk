# Shared Mailbox Missing or Cannot Send As

## Shared mailbox does not appear in Outlook
1. Access is granted by the mailbox owner, normally your team lead. Confirm with them that you were added.
2. New access takes up to 60 minutes to reach Outlook. Close Outlook completely and reopen it after that time.
3. If it still does not appear, add it yourself: File, Account Settings, double-click your account, More Settings, Advanced, Add, then type the shared mailbox name.
4. In Outlook on the web, right-click "Folders", choose "Add shared folder or mailbox" and type the mailbox address.

## "You do not have permission to send as this sender"
1. Being able to read a shared mailbox does not include sending from it; "Send As" is a separate permission. Ask the mailbox owner to request it.
2. When sending, pick the shared address in the From field. If the From field is hidden, enable it from the Options tab of the new message.
3. Outlook can cache an old address list. After permission is granted, remove the shared address from the From drop-down list and type it again.

## Sent items stay in your own mailbox
This is the default. The Email & Collaboration Team can change it so copies are saved in the shared mailbox's Sent Items; raise a ticket naming the mailbox.

## When to escalate
Escalate when the owner confirms access was granted more than two hours ago and the mailbox still cannot be added, or when a whole team loses a shared mailbox at once.
