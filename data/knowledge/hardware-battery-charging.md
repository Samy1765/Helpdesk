# Laptop Not Charging or Battery Draining Fast

## Not charging
1. Check the charger light. If the light is off, try another wall socket and reseat both ends of the cable (the brick has a detachable mains lead).
2. Use the charger that came with the laptop. Phone chargers and many USB-C hubs cannot supply enough power, and Windows will show "Slow charger".
3. Charge directly from the laptop's own USB-C port instead of through a monitor or dock to rule out the dock.
4. Shut the laptop down fully, hold the power button for 15 seconds, then connect the charger and start it again.
5. If the battery icon shows "Plugged in, not charging" at around 80 %, that is battery protection working as designed, not a fault.

## Battery drains quickly
1. Open Battery settings and check "Battery usage per app"; browsers with many tabs and video calls use the most.
2. Lower screen brightness and close apps that you are not using.
3. Make sure the laptop sleeps when the lid closes (Power settings, "Lid close action").
4. A battery older than three years normally holds about 70 % of its original charge. Hardware Support can run a battery health report.

## Safety
Stop using the laptop and contact Hardware Support immediately if the battery is swollen (the case bulges or the trackpad lifts), the laptop is unusually hot, or you smell burning. Do not charge it.

## When to escalate
Raise a ticket for Hardware Support with the laptop's asset tag if a second known-good charger does not charge it, or if the battery health report shows less than 60 % capacity.
