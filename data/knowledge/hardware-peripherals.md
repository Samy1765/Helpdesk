# Keyboard, Mouse, Headset and Webcam Problems

## Wireless keyboard or mouse stops responding
1. Replace or recharge the batteries; a low battery often causes lag before the device stops completely.
2. For devices with a USB receiver, move the receiver to a port on the front of the dock or laptop, away from metal and other receivers.
3. For Bluetooth devices, remove the device in Bluetooth settings and pair it again.

## Headset microphone or speakers not working in calls
1. In Teams or Zoom, open the device settings and select the headset for both speaker and microphone.
2. Check the mute switch on the headset cable or earcup.
3. In Windows Sound settings, open the microphone's properties and make sure it is not disabled and the level is above 50.
4. Under Privacy & security, then Microphone, make sure desktop apps are allowed to use the microphone.

## Webcam shows a black image
1. Check for a physical privacy shutter or a camera key on the keyboard (often F8 or F10).
2. Close every other app that might be using the camera; only one app can use it at a time.
3. Under Privacy & security, then Camera, allow desktop apps to access the camera.
4. Restart the laptop.

## Replacements
Standard keyboards, mice and headsets are replaced from the IT store at no charge; raise a hardware request. Bring the faulty item to the IT desk so it can be recycled.
