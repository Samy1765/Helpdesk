# Self-Service Password Reset and Registration

## Before you need it
Self-service reset only works if you registered at least two verification methods (authenticator app, mobile number or personal email). Check them once a year on the "Security info" page of your account.

## Resetting your password
1. Open the sign-in page and choose "Forgot my password".
2. Enter your company email address and complete the picture check.
3. Verify with two of your registered methods.
4. Choose a new password: at least 12 characters, not one of your last 10, and not containing your name or the company name. A short sentence is easier to remember than a random word.
5. Sign in on your laptop first while it is connected to the office network or the VPN, so the new password is cached for offline use.
6. Update the password on your phone's mail app and on saved Wi-Fi profiles.

## If the reset page says you are not registered
You will need help from IT. Ask the helpdesk agent to send a reset link to your registered personal email (needs your approval). If nothing is registered, IT has to verify your identity by video call with your manager present.

## Never
- Keep your password to yourself, even if the request seems to come from IT. IT will never ask for it.
- Never reuse your company password on other websites.
