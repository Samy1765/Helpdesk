# Report a suspicious email (you did not click anything)

Reporting takes ten seconds and removes the same email from everyone else's mailbox. IT will never ask for your password or an Authenticator code by email, chat or phone.

## Symptoms
- An email asks you to "verify your mailbox", "re-activate MFA" or open an invoice you did not expect.
- The sender's name looks internal but the address is external (for example, it-support@acme-demo-help.example).
- The email creates urgency: payment today, account closure, a parcel on hold.

## Resolution
1. Do not click links, open attachments or reply.
2. In Outlook, select the email and choose Report > Report phishing on the Home ribbon (on mobile: the three dots > Report phishing).
3. If the Report button is missing, forward the email as an attachment to phishing@acme-demo.in.
4. Delete the email from your Inbox after reporting it.
5. If colleagues received the same email, ask them to report it too rather than forward it around.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to the Security Team immediately when:
- You clicked a link, entered your password or opened an attachment (see "Clicked a phishing link or entered your password").
- The email impersonates a director asking for a payment or bank detail change.
- Many people received the same email within a short time.
