# Passwords, Account Lockouts and MFA

## Forgotten or expired password
1. Go to the self-service password portal from any browser (it is linked on the sign-in page as "Forgot password").
2. Verify your identity with your registered MFA method.
3. Choose a new password of at least 12 characters that you have not used before.
4. Sign out and back in on your laptop, phone mail app and VPN client so they pick up the new password.
Never share your password or MFA codes with anyone, including IT staff.

## Account locked out
Accounts lock for 15 minutes after 10 failed sign-in attempts.

1. Wait 15 minutes, or ask the helpdesk agent to unlock the account (requires your approval).
2. Find the device that keeps using an old password: phones with company mail and saved Wi-Fi profiles are the usual cause.
3. Update the password on that device, or remove and re-add the account.

## MFA / authenticator problems
1. If you have a new phone, use "Set up a new device" in the security info page while signed in on your laptop.
2. If you lost access to every MFA method, IT must verify your identity by video call before resetting MFA - this cannot be done by chat.
3. Make sure your phone's time is set automatically; time drift breaks one-time codes.
