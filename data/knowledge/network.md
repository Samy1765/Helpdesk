# Network and Wi-Fi

## Wi-Fi connected but no internet
1. Turn Wi-Fi off and on again, then forget the CORP-WIFI network and reconnect with your company credentials.
2. Check whether colleagues near you have the same problem; if so, report the area and floor.
3. Flush the DNS cache on your device (the helpdesk agent can do this with your approval).
4. Restart your laptop to renew its network address.

## Slow network
1. Run a speed test from the company intranet speed test page and include the result in your ticket.
2. Close large downloads, cloud sync pauses and video streams you do not need.
3. Prefer a wired connection or move closer to an access point; walls and floors weaken Wi-Fi.

## Wired connection not working
1. Check the cable is clicked into both the laptop/dock and the wall port; try another port or cable.
2. Look for link lights on the network port; no lights usually means a bad cable or port.
3. If you use a docking station, disconnect and reconnect the dock, then restart.
