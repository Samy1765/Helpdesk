# Docking Station and External Monitors Not Detected

## Symptoms
- External monitors stay black or show "No signal" when the laptop is docked.
- Only one of two monitors works.
- Keyboard, mouse or wired network on the dock stop working.

## Steps
1. Check the monitor's input source button: it must match the cable you use (DisplayPort, HDMI or USB-C).
2. Unplug the dock from its power supply, wait 10 seconds and plug it back in. Then reconnect the laptop.
3. Press Windows key + P and choose "Extend".
4. Open Display settings and press "Detect". Arrange the screens so they match your desk layout.
5. Connect one monitor directly to the laptop. If it works, the dock or its cable is the problem; if not, check the monitor and cable.
6. Install pending driver and firmware updates from Software Center ("Dock firmware" and "Graphics driver"), then restart.

## Hot-desking floors
Docks on shared desks sometimes have a firmware version that does not support two monitors with every laptop model. Use a desk marked "Dual screen" or report the desk number.

## When to escalate
Raise a ticket for Hardware Support with the dock's asset tag and desk number if the dock still fails after a power cycle and updates. Swapping a dock is usually done the same day.
