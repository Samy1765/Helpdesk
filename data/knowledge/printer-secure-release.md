# Secure Print Release with Your Badge

## How secure printing works
Every job goes to the "FollowMe" queue and is held until you tap your badge on any office printer. Jobs that are not released within 24 hours are deleted. This keeps confidential documents from sitting in output trays.

## Badge is not recognised
1. Tap the badge flat on the reader and hold it for two seconds.
2. If the screen says "Unknown card", register the badge: choose "Register card" on the printer, sign in with your company username and password, then tap the badge again. Registration is needed once per badge.
3. A replacement badge always needs to be registered again.

## Job does not appear at the printer
1. Check that you printed to "FollowMe", not to a specific printer name.
2. Wait 30 seconds; large PDFs take time to process.
3. Check the job was not deleted for being older than 24 hours.
4. Colour jobs only appear on colour printers, and some departments are limited to black and white.

## Releasing printed jobs at another office
The FollowMe queue works at every office. Your jobs follow you to whichever printer you badge in at.

## When to escalate
Raise a ticket for Print Services with the printer's asset number (on the front panel) if the reader does not respond at all, or if registered badges stop working for everyone at that printer.
