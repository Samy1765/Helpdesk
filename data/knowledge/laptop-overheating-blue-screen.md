# Laptop overheats, fan is loud or the screen turns blue

Heat and blue screens are often caused by blocked vents or a driver update that did not finish. A laptop that crashes repeatedly needs a hardware check, because data on it may be at risk.

## Symptoms
- The fan runs loudly all the time and the base is hot to touch.
- The laptop shuts down without warning.
- A blue screen with a stop code appears and the laptop restarts.

## Resolution
1. Use the laptop on a hard, flat surface; beds, cushions and laps block the vents.
2. Open Task Manager and check whether one application uses most of the CPU; close it and see whether the fan calms down.
3. Install pending updates from Company Portal > Updates and restart, even if no restart was requested.
4. Write down the stop code shown on the blue screen (for example, DRIVER_POWER_STATE_FAILURE) before it restarts.
5. Make sure your work files are saved to OneDrive so nothing is lost if the laptop needs repair.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to Hardware Support when:
- Blue screens happen more than once in a day; include the stop code.
- The laptop switches off from heat even on a flat surface.
- The case is cracked, the battery is bulging or there is a burning smell; stop using the laptop.
