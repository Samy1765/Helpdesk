# Lost or stolen laptop or phone with company data

Company laptops are encrypted with BitLocker and phones with work email can be wiped remotely, so a quick report keeps company data safe.

## Symptoms
- A company laptop, phone or security key is missing.
- A device was taken in a theft or left in a taxi, train or airport.

## Resolution
1. Report the loss as soon as you notice it, with the time and place it was last seen.
2. Change your password at https://myaccount.acme-demo.in from another device and choose "Sign out everywhere".
3. For a phone, remove it from Security info at https://myaccount.acme-demo.in so it can no longer approve sign-ins.
4. For theft, file a police report and attach the report number to the ticket; the insurer needs it.
5. Hardware Support arranges a replacement laptop once the Security Team has secured the old one.

## Escalation criteria
Always escalated to the Security Team, which locks and wipes the device. Tell them at once when:
- The device was unlocked when it was lost.
- It held customer data downloaded outside OneDrive or SharePoint.
