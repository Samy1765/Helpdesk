# Scan to Email and Scan to Folder Not Working

## Scan to email does not arrive
1. On the printer, choose "Scan to me" after badging in; this always sends to your own address and avoids typing errors.
2. Check the Junk Email folder. Scans come from the printer's own address, which some rules filter.
3. Large scans can exceed the 20 MB attachment limit. Scan in black and white, at 200 dpi, or split the document.
4. Wait five minutes; the printer queues scans when the mail service is busy.

## Scan to folder fails
1. Choose "Scan to my folder". Scans are saved to the "Scans" folder in your OneDrive, not to a network drive.
2. If the printer reports "Authentication failed", sign out at the printer and badge in again.
3. Check that your OneDrive is not full.

## Scan quality is poor
1. Clean the scanner glass and the narrow strip used by the document feeder with a dry, lint-free cloth. Streaks across every page usually come from dirt on that strip.
2. Remove staples and paper clips before using the document feeder.

## When to escalate
Raise a ticket for Print Services with the printer's asset number if no scans from that printer arrive for anyone, or if the feeder jams on every scan.
