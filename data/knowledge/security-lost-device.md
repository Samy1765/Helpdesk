# Lost or Stolen Laptop or Phone

## Report immediately
Report a lost or stolen company device, or a personal phone with company email on it, as soon as you notice, even outside office hours. Raise a Security ticket marked critical or call the IT emergency number on the back of your badge.

Include:
1. Which device (laptop, phone, tablet) and its asset tag if you know it.
2. When and where you last had it.
3. Whether it was locked and whether it was signed in to company apps.

## What IT does
1. Signs you out of company apps on that device and blocks it from connecting.
2. Locates the device if location services are on.
3. Remotely wipes company data. On personal phones only the company apps and data are removed, never your personal photos or files.
4. Issues a replacement laptop, normally within two working days.

## What you should do
1. Change your company password from another device.
2. For theft, file a police report and send the reference number to the Security Team; insurance requires it.
3. If the device is found later, do not use it. Return it to IT so it can be checked and re-enrolled.

## Prevention
Laptops are encrypted, so data stays protected if the screen is locked. Always lock the screen (Windows key + L) when you leave the laptop, and never leave devices visible in a car.
