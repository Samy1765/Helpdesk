# Office, Acrobat or AutoCAD says it is unlicensed

Licences are assigned to your account, not the laptop. "Unlicensed product" banners appear when the app is signed in with a personal account or when a licence was moved during a team change.

## Symptoms
- Word or Excel shows a red "Unlicensed Product" bar or opens in read-only mode.
- Acrobat asks you to start a free trial.
- AutoCAD says "Your access has expired" or "No licence available".

## Resolution
1. In the app, open File > Account (Office) or Help > Sign in (Acrobat, AutoCAD) and check the signed-in address is your name@acme-demo.in account.
2. Sign out of any personal account, close all Office or Adobe apps, then open one and sign in with your company account.
3. For Office, choose File > Account > Update License after signing in.
4. Restart the laptop if the banner remains, so the licence token is refreshed.
5. If you changed teams recently, check in the Company Portal under "My software" whether the licence is still assigned to you.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to Software Support when:
- The licence is not listed under "My software" and you still need it (manager approval required).
- The app keeps asking for a trial after signing in with your company account.
- Several people in one team lose a licence on the same day (possible renewal problem).
