# Teams meetings - others cannot hear you or the camera is black

Teams often picks the wrong microphone or speaker after a headset or dock is plugged in during a call. A camera privacy shutter or another app holding the camera causes a black video tile.

## Symptoms
- Colleagues say they cannot hear you, or you hear them through the laptop speakers instead of the headset.
- The camera preview is black or shows "Camera not found".
- Audio is robotic or cuts out on every call.

## Resolution
1. In the meeting, open More > Settings > Device settings and select the headset for both speaker and microphone.
2. Unplug the headset, wait five seconds and plug it into a USB port directly on the laptop rather than the dock.
3. Check the physical camera shutter or the F8 camera key on Dell and Lenovo laptops.
4. Close other apps that use the camera, such as Zoom or the Windows Camera app, then rejoin the meeting.
5. For robotic audio, turn off your video and close other downloads; poor bandwidth affects audio first.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to the Email & Collaboration Team when:
- No audio device appears in Teams at all after a restart.
- Several people in the same meeting room have audio problems (the room system may need a check).
Hardware Support handles headsets and webcams that are not detected by Windows itself.
