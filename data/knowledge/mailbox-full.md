# Mailbox is full or close to its size limit

At 49 GB Outlook warns you; at 50 GB you can no longer send mail. Online archiving is switched on for every mailbox and moves mail older than two years automatically.

## Symptoms
- A yellow bar in Outlook says "Your mailbox is almost full".
- New messages bounce back to senders with "mailbox full".
- You cannot send, and Outlook shows "Mailbox size limit exceeded".

## Resolution
1. In Outlook, go to File > Tools > Mailbox Cleanup and choose "View Mailbox Size" to find the largest folders.
2. Sort the Sent Items and Inbox by Size and save large attachments to OneDrive, then delete the messages.
3. Empty Deleted Items and the Recoverable Items folder (Folder tab > Recover Deleted Items shows what remains).
4. Move older project folders to your Online Archive; drag them onto "Online Archive" in the folder list.
5. Check the size again after 30 minutes; the figure updates with a delay.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to the Email & Collaboration Team when:
- The mailbox is still over the limit after clean-up and you cannot send business-critical mail.
- The Online Archive is missing from the folder list.
