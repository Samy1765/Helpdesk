# Shared mailbox or team calendar is missing or out of date

Shared mailboxes appear in Outlook automatically up to two hours after access is granted. Calendars that stop updating usually need the offline copy cleared.

## Symptoms
- A shared mailbox you were given access to does not appear in the Outlook folder list.
- Meeting invites appear on your phone but not in Outlook on the laptop.
- A colleague's calendar shows only "Busy" blocks when you expected details.

## Resolution
1. Wait two hours after access is granted, then close and reopen Outlook.
2. To add a shared mailbox by hand: File > Account Settings > Account Settings > Change > More Settings > Advanced > Add, and type the mailbox address.
3. For a calendar that is out of date, right-click it, choose Properties > Clear Offline Items, then press Send/Receive.
4. Sign out of Teams and back in if Teams meetings do not appear in the Teams calendar.
5. Ask the calendar owner to share it with "Can view all details" if you only see Busy blocks.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to the Email & Collaboration Team when:
- The shared mailbox is still missing 24 hours after access was approved.
- A shared mailbox shows "You don't have permission" for sending; send-as rights need a separate approval.
- Calendars stop updating for a whole team.
