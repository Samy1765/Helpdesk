# Microsoft 365 Apps Show "Unlicensed Product" or Ask to Activate

## Symptoms
- A red bar in Word, Excel or Outlook says "Unlicensed Product" or "Account error".
- Editing is disabled and documents open read-only.

## Steps
1. In any Office app, open File, then Account, and check which account is signed in under "User Information". It must be your company account, not a personal Microsoft account.
2. If a personal account is shown, sign out of it, close all Office apps and sign in again with your company email.
3. Close every Office app, including Teams and OneDrive, then reopen Word and choose "Sign in" if prompted.
4. Make sure the laptop has internet access; licences are checked online at least once every 30 days.
5. Ask the helpdesk agent to clear the Office application cache (needs your approval). This removes stored licence and account tokens so a fresh sign-in can complete.
6. Restart the laptop.

## Licence removed after a role change
Licences follow your job role. If you recently moved team, the new licence may still be pending; your manager can confirm in the access request.

## When to escalate
Escalate to Software Support if the account page shows your company account but still says "Unlicensed", which usually means the licence is not assigned to you.
