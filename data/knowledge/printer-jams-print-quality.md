# Paper jams, streaks or faded prints

The printer's own screen shows where a jam is. Faded or streaky prints usually mean low toner or a dirty glass on the scanner, not a broken printer.

## Symptoms
- The printer panel shows a jam in tray 2 or the rear door.
- Prints have vertical streaks or faded areas.
- Copies show lines that printed documents do not.

## Resolution
1. Follow the animation on the printer panel; open only the doors it shows and pull jammed paper out slowly in the direction of the paper path.
2. Fan the paper stack and reload it; damp or curled paper jams more often.
3. For lines on copies only, wipe the narrow glass strip on the left of the scanner glass with a dry cloth.
4. Check the toner level on the printer panel (Menu > Supplies). Do not shake or open toner cartridges yourself.
5. Use another printer through Acme Secure Print; your job can be released at any device.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to Print Services when:
- A jam code remains after the paper is removed, or paper is torn inside the printer.
- Toner is below 10% or prints stay faded with full toner.
- The printer makes grinding noises; switch it off and put a "Do not use" note on it.
