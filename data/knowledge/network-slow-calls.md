# Slow Network, Choppy Video Calls and Lag

## Symptoms
- Teams or Zoom calls freeze, the audio cuts out, or the app shows "Your network quality is affecting the call".
- File downloads from SharePoint or the file server are much slower than usual.

## First checks (two minutes)
1. Run the speed test linked on the IT portal. Under 10 Mbps down or 3 Mbps up is too slow for video.
2. Close cloud sync tools that are uploading large files (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox) during the call.
3. Turn off your own video; audio-only needs far less bandwidth.

## In the office
1. Plug into a wired port at your desk if one is available; Wi-Fi in meeting rooms is shared by many people.
2. Move away from the kitchen and lift lobbies, where coverage is weakest.
3. Note the time and the room; if several people report the same area the Network Team checks the access point.

## At home
1. Move closer to the router, or use the 5 GHz network if your router offers one.
2. Ask other people at home to pause streaming or large downloads during important calls.
3. Restart the router if it has been on for weeks.
4. If calls are only bad while on the VPN, disconnect the VPN for Teams calls: Teams does not need it.

## When to escalate
Escalate to the Network Team when a speed test on a wired office port is below 50 Mbps, or when a whole team or floor is affected at the same time.
