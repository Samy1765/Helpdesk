# Laptop will not power on or charge

A laptop that seems dead is often just a drained battery behind a faulty charger or a dock that does not supply enough power. Check power before assuming a hardware fault.

## Symptoms
- Pressing the power button does nothing; no lights or fan.
- The battery icon says "Plugged in, not charging".
- The laptop only works when connected to a particular dock.

## Resolution
1. Connect the original 65 W charger directly to the laptop (not through the dock) and leave it for 30 minutes.
2. Check the charging light next to the USB-C port; Dell shows white, Lenovo shows amber while charging.
3. Hold the power button for 20 seconds to reset the power controller, then press it once normally.
4. Try a colleague's charger of the same model to rule out the adapter.
5. Remove the laptop from any dock while testing; some docks cannot start a fully drained laptop.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to Hardware Support when:
- The laptop does not start after 30 minutes on a known good charger.
- The battery is swollen, the case is bent or the laptop smells of burning; stop using it immediately.
- A replacement charger is needed (Hardware Support keeps spares at each site).
