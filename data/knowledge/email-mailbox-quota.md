# Mailbox Full or Close to Its Size Limit

## Limits
- Standard mailbox: 50 GB. A warning appears at 49 GB, sending stops at 49.5 GB and receiving stops at 50 GB.
- Online archive: up to 100 GB, included for every employee.

## Free up space quickly
1. In Outlook, right-click your mailbox name and open "Data File Properties", then "Folder Size", to see which folders are largest.
2. Sort the Inbox and Sent Items by size and delete or archive messages with large attachments. Save attachments you need to OneDrive first.
3. Empty "Deleted Items" and then "Recoverable Items" (Folder tab, "Recover Deleted Items", select all, "Purge").
4. Move older mail to the "Online Archive" folder. Mail in the archive stays searchable but does not count towards the mailbox limit.

## Make it automatic
Apply the "Archive after 1 year" policy to your Inbox (right-click the folder, "Assign Policy"). Mail older than a year then moves to the archive every night.

## When to escalate
Raise a ticket for the Email & Collaboration Team if the Online Archive folder is missing from your mailbox, or if the mailbox is still full after archiving because it belongs to a role that legally needs to keep all mail.
