# Add the office printer or fix a printer shown as offline

Most people only need one printer, Acme Secure Print, which works at every office. Direct printers (plant label printers, plotters) are added from the Company Portal.

## Symptoms
- Acme Secure Print is missing from the printer list on a new laptop.
- The printer shows "Offline" in Printers & scanners.
- A plant label printer or plotter is not in the list.

## Resolution
1. Open the Company Portal app, search for "Acme Secure Print" and choose Install. It takes about two minutes.
2. On macOS, open Self Service and install "Acme Secure Print (Mac)".
3. If the printer shows Offline, connect to the office network or VPN; the queue is reachable only inside the company network.
4. Remove old printers you no longer use (right-click > Remove device) so applications do not default to them.
5. For plant label printers and plotters, search the Company Portal for the printer name written on its front panel.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to Print Services when:
- The Company Portal installation fails with an error.
- A plant or plotter printer is not listed in the Company Portal.
- Acme Secure Print stays offline while you are connected to the office network.
