# Security Incidents and Phishing

## Reporting a suspicious or phishing email (you did not click)
1. Do not click links, open attachments or reply.
2. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook; this sends the message to the Security team and removes it from your inbox.
3. If the email pretends to be from a colleague or supplier, warn them through a separate channel.

## You clicked a link, opened an attachment or entered your password
1. Disconnect from the network (turn off Wi-Fi or unplug the cable) but do not power off the computer.
2. Do not delete anything; evidence helps the investigation.
3. Change your password from a different, trusted device using the self-service portal.
4. Stay available: the Security team will contact you. This always requires a human investigation.

## Suspected malware or ransomware
Signs include files that suddenly will not open or have strange extensions, ransom notes, or constant pop-ups.
1. Disconnect from the network immediately and leave the computer on.
2. Report it at once; the Security team handles every malware case.
