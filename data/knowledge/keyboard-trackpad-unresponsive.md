# Laptop keyboard, trackpad or USB mouse stops responding

Input devices most often stop working because of a flat battery, a USB receiver moved to a different port, or the trackpad turned off with a function key.

## Symptoms
- Some keys do nothing, or type the wrong characters.
- The trackpad does not move the pointer, but a USB mouse works.
- A wireless keyboard or mouse works for a few seconds, then stops.

## Resolution
1. For wireless sets, replace the batteries and plug the USB receiver into a port directly on the laptop.
2. Press the trackpad function key (F6 on Dell, F11 on some Lenovo models) or check Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Touchpad is On.
3. If the keyboard types the wrong characters, press Windows key + Space and select English (India) as the input language.
4. Restart the laptop; a stuck driver is reset at start-up.
5. Clean around the keys with dry compressed air only; do not use liquids.

## Escalation criteria
Escalate to Hardware Support when:
- The built-in keyboard or trackpad still fails after a restart.
- Liquid was spilled on the keyboard; switch the laptop off, unplug it and bring it to the service desk.
- Keys are physically broken or missing.
