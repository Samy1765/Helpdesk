# Printer and Scanning

## Print jobs stuck in the queue
1. Open Settings > Printers & scanners, select the printer and choose Open print queue.
2. Cancel all documents in the queue.
3. Restart the print spooler on your device (the helpdesk agent can do this with your approval).
4. Print a test page. If jobs still do not print, try another floor printer to check whether the printer itself is at fault.

## Paper jam or error light
1. Open the trays and front panel as shown on the printer display and gently remove any paper, pulling in the direction of the paper path.
2. Close all panels firmly; the printer will not resume with a panel open.
3. If the display shows a service code (for example SC-542), note it and report it - do not open the fuser unit, it is hot.

## Printer shows offline or needs to be added
1. Make sure you are connected to the office network or VPN; printers are not reachable from guest Wi-Fi.
2. Remove the printer from Printers & scanners and add it again from the company print portal ("Add printer" > choose your floor).
3. Install the driver offered by the print portal; do not download drivers from third-party websites.
